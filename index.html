About

I am a PhD student at the Department of Psychiatry and Psychotherapy at Philipps-Universität Marburg. My research focuses on how speech and language relate to psychopathology, cognitive functioning, and brain structure in affective and psychotic disorders.

I use computational approaches to examine patterns in spontaneous speech and explore how they relate to clinical symptoms and neuroimaging measures. My work combines natural language processing, acoustic speech analysis, statistical methods, and machine learning, with a particular interest in dimensions of psychopathology that extend beyond diagnostic categories.

Research interests

Computational psychiatryTransdiagnostic psychopathology, cognition, and quantitative approaches to psychiatric research.
